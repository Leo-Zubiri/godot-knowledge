> Preserved pointer + key points (conventionally immutable). **Basis:** pages fetched on 2026-11-08 and summarized by a fetch-time summarizer — a **paraphrased extraction, not verbatim legal text**. Terms change; the version in force at *your download date* may govern (e.g. QAL, Sonniss). **Not legal advice.** Items marked `unverified` were not read on an official page (403/404/not fetched). Licence deeds are plain-language summaries with no legal value — the full legal code governs. Re-fetch and have a human read the actual terms before relying on any asset.

- **Freesound licences (FAQ, read):** per-sound CC0 (public-domain-like; cannot claim you created it), CC-BY (credit required), CC-BY-NC (credit, **no** commercial use), Sampling+ legacy (roughly NC plus no advertising use; being phased out). Derivative rules: CC0 source can be remixed under CC0, CC-BY or CC-BY-NC; CC-BY source under CC-BY or CC-BY-NC with credit (not CC0); CC-BY-NC source only under CC-BY-NC. Attribution template names sound, author, link and licence. Uploaders can add **generative-AI preferences** on top (none; open-source models only; non-commercial open-source only; no generative AI), plus an opt-out for solo speech recordings. Bulk downloads for AI training go through the Data Packs Portal, not the API.
- **Freesound API terms (read):** one key per application; allowed to retrieve, process and display content within each sound's licence; temporary copies only as needed and then deleted; **forbidden:** sharing API access, building a substitute service, full database copies, bots/spiders/scraping beyond the API, violating content licences; commercial API access negotiated case by case; Freesound may limit volume or introduce charges.
- **Freesound API v2 (read):** `license` and `gen_ai_preference` are search filters and instance fields; downloads (single sound or pack zip) require **OAuth2**; a usage endpoint reports requests in the last minute and 24 h with limits (limits are per user).
- **Sonniss GDC bundles (page + full licence, read):** free yearly bundles 2015–2024 (2021–2023 combined; no 2025 bundle; a 2026 bundle has its own page). Licence: worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, lifetime, unlimited projects; commercial use in games, films, apps etc. and selling the finished project; may edit/redesign but not claim authorship of originals; no credit required; **may not distribute, publish, sublicense or supply the sounds as sound effects** to others, original or edited, including in libraries, sample packs, asset packs, templates and SDKs (finished projects exempt); sharing allowed with colleagues in your own team/organization working on your projects; **may not use the sounds to train or develop AI technology**, including tools that generate similar sound effects; no termination clause; the version in effect on your download date applies, newer versions not retroactive; English law.
- **incompetech (FAQ, read):** most tracks CC BY 4.0 (monetised YouTube etc. allowed with credit); a paid Standard License exists for cases where credit is impossible. Credit must be findable (may sit behind a reasonable step such as a credits menu) and include track title, composer (Kevin MacLeod), incompetech.com, the licence name and link; note which parts you altered if you changed it (plain cutting/splicing needs no mention). For games, credits usually go on a credits screen. Keep a printed licence text and a printout of the track page as proof. Content ID is not explained on the page.
- **Pixabay (license summary, read):** free use without credit; may modify and build on; **prohibited:** selling or sharing media in original form unless you added your own creative work; commercial use of recognizable brands/logos; immoral/illegal/misleading uses; use as part of a trademark or business name; third-party rights may still apply; the summary defers to the binding Terms of Service and does not say how the licence may change (the research agent reported terms prohibiting scraping/bots and ML-purpose scraping — from the terms page, not re-read here).
