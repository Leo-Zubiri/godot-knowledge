- **Pack page (read):** states the pack is released under **CC0** and may be used in personal, educational and commercial projects; no usage restrictions beyond CC0 are stated and attribution is not mentioned. Tiers: **Standard (free)** ~60–70% of the pack in OBJ, FBX and glTF; **Pro** adds ~30–40% more in the same formats and is also free to use; **Source** includes all models implemented for Unreal, Unity (URP) and Godot with custom shaders plus `.BLEND` sources, accessed through Patreon pledges ($10/$20/$50 per month = 15/30/75 claim credits redeemed via Discord). The formats list names FBX, GLB and Blend (overlapping the tier wording). Animations were built on a **universal humanoid rig** meant to be retargeted; the page says the custom exports work in and were tested in Godot, Unity and Unreal (vendor claim, not independently verified).
- **Conflict (unresolved):** the main Quaternius licence page (read earlier, see src-gs-asset-packs-3d-2d) is the custom **Quaternius Asset License (QAL) v1.0**, which is not CC0 and forbids redistributing the Assets as a standalone pack. The pack page says CC0. Which governs this pack is a question for human review; until settled, treat it as conditional, keep both statements, and do not redistribute the original files.
