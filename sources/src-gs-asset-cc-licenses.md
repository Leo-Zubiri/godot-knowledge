- **CC0 1.0:** public-domain dedication; copy, modify, distribute, perform, including commercially, no permission needed. Does **not** affect patent or trademark rights, nor others' rights such as publicity or privacy that may still limit use. No warranties; do not imply the author endorses your use. Fallback licence not described on the deed page (see legal code).
- **CC BY 4.0:** copy, redistribute, remix, build on, for any purpose including commercial. Must credit the creator, link the licence, state whether changes were made (4.0: say you altered it and keep a record of earlier modifications), without implying endorsement. Licensor cannot revoke while you comply. **No additional restrictions**: no added legal terms or technical controls that stop others doing what the licence allows. Deed does not address AI training (contested elsewhere; flag for human review).
- **CC BY-SA 4.0:** as BY, plus **ShareAlike**: if you alter or build on the work, release your version under the same licence or a CC-listed compatible one; "adapted material" = anything made by changing, reworking or extending the original; no additional restrictions (including technical measures). Whether ShareAlike reaches a whole game or only the adapted asset is **an open question for human/legal review** — the deed points to the legal code for edge cases.
- **CC BY-NC 4.0:** NonCommercial = not primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation; so unsuitable for a commercial game. Attribution (name, copyright and licence notices, disclaimer, link), modification allowed with change record, redistribution allowed non-commercially, no added restrictions.
- All four deeds state they are summaries without legal value; the full licence text governs.
