- **Game-analysis methods named:** MDA-based analysis; Björk, Lundgren & Holopainen, "Game Design Patterns" (DiGRA 2003; reportedly 200+ patterns in ~11 groups); Lankoski & Björk, formal analysis of gameplay (*Game Research Methods*, ETC Press 2015); Bizzocchi & Tanenbaum, close playing (*Well Played 3.0*, 2011; page ranges and co-author name conflict across references); usability/playability heuristics — Pinelle, Wong & Stach (CHI 2008; ten heuristics; ACM page 403), Desurvire, Caplan & Toth (CHI 2004 EA; HEP; ~43 heuristics reported) and PLAY (~50 reported). Practitioner teardowns (GDC Vault descriptions only).
- **Fair-use pointer:** the statutory factor list of 17 USC §107 (purpose and character, nature of the work, amount and substantiality, effect on the market) via Copyright Office material; courts weigh them case by case. No legal conclusion is drawn; whether screenshots, extracted assets, datamined content or reproduced text are acceptable is a question for human/legal review, and datamining may also breach a game's terms (no specific EULA checked).
