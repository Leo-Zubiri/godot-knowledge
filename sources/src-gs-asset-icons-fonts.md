> Preserved pointer + key points (conventionally immutable). **Basis:** pages fetched on 2026-11-08 and summarized by a fetch-time summarizer — a **paraphrased extraction, not verbatim legal text**. Terms change; the version in force at *your download date* may govern (e.g. QAL, Sonniss). **Not legal advice.** Items marked `unverified` were not read on an official page (403/404/not fetched). Licence deeds are plain-language summaries with no legal value — the full legal code governs. Re-fetch and have a human read the actual terms before relying on any asset.

- **Lucide (read):** ISC licence (© Lucide Icons and contributors); a listed set of Feather-derived icons is MIT (© Cole Bemis); both require keeping the copyright and permission notices with copies; commercial use unrestricted; no warranty. Package availability (npm/SVG) `unverified`.
- **Phosphor (core repo README, per research agent):** MIT, repo-wide; keep the MIT notice. **Tabler Icons (site, per research agent):** MIT, no attribution needed; the full licence page (tabler.io/license) was not read.
- **Font Awesome Free (LICENSE.txt, read):** icons (SVG/JS) CC BY 4.0, fonts SIL OFL 1.1, code MIT; icon files already carry attribution comments that should be left in place; brand logos belong to their owners and should only be used to represent that company/product/service. Pro tier not researched.
- **SIL OFL (openfontlicense.org, read):** OFL fonts may be used in print, artwork, logos and websites without crediting the designer; fonts can be bundled in software or apps **subject to conditions the page does not list** (see its how-to guide); modification and redistribution allowed, but a modified font may need renaming if a Reserved Font Name is declared; designers keep copyright. Whether the OFL text must travel with embedded fonts in a game: not stated on the page — read the full OFL.
- **Google Fonts:** official FAQ pages returned only a topic list; terms `unverified` from official pages (most families are OFL but check each family's licence file).
