> Preserved excerpt (conventionally immutable). One-page rules sheet of a professional pool tour; event rules, not the world governing body's rulebook (which was not read). Useful as a real-world reference for turn-timer values in a turn-based cue-sport game.

- "A 30-second Shot Clock will be in operation."
- "The Shot Clock will start when the last moving ball comes to rest and the Official is satisfied that the previous turn has finished"
- "Each side is allowed one 30-second extension per rack. This can be called at any time within the initial 30-seconds verbally (!) by the Shooter and must be acknowledged by the Official. The number of available extensions will be visualised in the arena."
- "After the break-off shot, the Shooter can take a maximum of 60 seconds."
- "After a Push Out has been played, the incoming side can take a maximum of 60 seconds."
- "If there are no Shot Clock sound warnings in the arena, the Official will alert Players when 10 and 5 seconds are remaining on the Shot Clock."
- "The Shot Clock will be stopped, when the Shooter strikes the cue-ball."
- "Failure to play a shot in the allocated time will result in a Standard Foul."
- "The Official can stop the Shot Clock, if they deem to necessary (e.g. outside influences)."
