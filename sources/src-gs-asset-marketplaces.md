- **Fab (fab.com/eula, summary page only):** Standard License lets you use assets for commercial or private work, modify them, distribute your projects commercially with the assets incorporated, use them with compatible tools (not only Unreal Engine), and **share assets with collaborators on the same project, including via a private repository**; you cannot resell or give away an asset on its own or allow others to; Personal and Professional tiers grant the same rights; Reference-Only tier gives referenced form only, not source format; no credit required; CC-BY listings are governed by CC BY instead. The page states it is only a summary and the **full Fab EULA is the binding document** (not read); AI training/generative AI, termination and changes to terms not covered. `fab.com/licenses` returned 404. Per-listing formats and API: `unverified`.
- **Sketchfab (download-api guidelines + developer terms, read):** downloads require a Sketchfab account (end users sign in via OAuth 2.0; apps needing downloads without user sign-in must contact Sketchfab); over 700,000 models are CC-licensed and most allow commercial use (individual licence types not listed on the page; the licences help page returned 404 earlier); apps must show each model's licence and author, and the credit must stay visible to downstream users; apps must state models come from Sketchfab (Developer terms §4.5–4.7); Sketchfab may change/restrict/stop the APIs without notice. Rate limits, automation rules and NoAI/AI restrictions are **not** on these pages (a NoAI tag exists per secondary sources, `unverified`).
- **itch.io (terms, read):** publishers keep ownership and must hold the rights; buyers get a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to access and use purchased content in platform-allowed ways, which **continues if the publisher later removes the content**; itch.io gets a worldwide licence to host/promote; content can be taken down on infringement notices (DMCA flow) and accounts terminated without notice; terms can change without notice; no dedicated bot/scraper rule, but harvesting information about other users is prohibited. Per-pack licence is defined by each creator (not on this page).
- **CGTrader:** homepage says licensing options vary (personal vs commercial) and to check each listing, the Terms and the Help Center; free-model licence terms were not retrieved (`unverified`).
- **OpenGameArt:** FAQ returned **403** (twice); licences in use, attribution and commercial rules `unverified` (per-asset licensing is prior knowledge only).
- **Mixamo (Adobe):** official FAQ returned **403**. Terms are `unverified` from the official site; search-snippet (secondary) claims — royalty-free incl. games, no redistribution of raw characters/animations to non-team members, no ML-training use — must not be relied on without reading the official text. No sanctioned public API found; automation would be GUI/scraping, a ToS risk.
