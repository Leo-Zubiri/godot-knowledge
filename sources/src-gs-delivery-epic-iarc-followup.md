## IARC (page read)
Developers complete one IARC questionnaire when preparing to publish on a participating storefront; IARC assigns regional age ratings, content descriptors and interactive elements (the page says these disclose in-game purchases including randomized ones, user interaction, location sharing and internet access); the storefront ingests and maps ratings for its region. Developers complete the questionnaire once and other IARC storefronts can use the certificate ID. Rating authorities monitor ratings; corrections go through storefront APIs. The page does not state who pays, timing, a dispute procedure or the list of participating authorities.

## Epic Games Store (search summary only, UNV)
A search summary of Epic's official pages says: 100%/0% revenue share on the first $1 million net revenue per product per year, then the standard 88%/12%; started 2025-06-01; from 2026 the threshold resets each 1 January; per title across platforms; automatic, no opt-in; recoupable obligations are recouped first; Epic's fee applies to payments Epic processes, own payment systems inside games incur no Epic fee; Epic First Run gives 100% net revenue on eligible products for the first six months of exclusivity and counts toward the same $1M threshold. The summary itself said it could not confirm the terms are still in effect for 2026.
